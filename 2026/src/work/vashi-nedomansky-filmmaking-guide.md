## Intro & Goal

The filmmaking guide was developed to serve as an organic social asset and lead generation tool, promoting Dell’s Precision workstations to media and entertainment SMEs. The objective: provide authentic, high-value content featuring Hollywood editor Vashi Nedomansky, to build credibility, drive marketing-ready leads, and challenge Apple’s dominance in the media production sector.

## Challenges

- Coordinating communication and approvals with a high-profile creative partner while maintaining strict delivery timelines.
- Managing permissions and licensing rights to use well-known intellectual property (e.g., Deadpool references).
- Navigating approval processes across Dell’s brand and legal departments.
- Balancing creative integrity with marketing requirements and brand guidelines.

## Solution

- Applied an iterative project management approach to tightly control approvals: locked content before progressing to design phases, minimizing risk of scope creep.
- Developed the primary output as an interactive HTML eGuide for U.S. campaigns.
- Produced a secondary interactive PDF variant tailored for EMEA content syndication programs.
- Maintained early, frequent collaboration with both Vashi’s team and Dell’s internal stakeholders to ensure aligned expectations and approvals.

## My Role

- Project managed the eGuide from kickoff through final delivery.
- Oversaw content development, creative production, and stakeholder coordination.
- Managed all internal and external approval rounds across creative and legal teams.
- Delivered the full project within the required funding and publishing deadlines.

## Results

- Produced a highly engaging eGuide that resonated with the target media and entertainment audience.
- Successfully approved for syndication in EMEA campaigns.
- Earned strong praise from the project sponsor and marketing teams.
